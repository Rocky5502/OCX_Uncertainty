# Background Questionnaire

Required fields:

1. Primary role: software engineer, AI/ML researcher, PhD student, other.
2. Additional applicable roles, multi-select.
3. Total years of programming experience, numeric.
4. Years of Python experience, numeric.
5. Code-review frequency: never, monthly or less, several times per month,
   weekly, several times per week, daily.
6. Repository-level development frequency using the same scale.
7. AI coding-tool usage: never, monthly or less, several times per month,
   weekly, several times per week, daily.
8. Familiarity with unit testing, 1--5.
9. Familiarity with repository dependency tracing, 1--5.

Do not ask for employer, exact job title, name, email, nationality, gender, or
other identifying attributes unless separately justified and approved.
