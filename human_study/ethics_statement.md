# Ethics and Data-Governance Statement

Recruitment is blocked until the principal investigator records the applicable
institutional ethics approval, exemption, or other formal determination.

The study involves adults reviewing public benchmark code. Participants will
provide informed consent, may withdraw without penalty, and will not be asked
to disclose private source code, employer information, credentials, or sensitive
personal data. Compensation, expected duration, study purpose, foreseeable
risks, and data-sharing plans must be disclosed before consent.

Participant codes are pseudonymous. Contact and compensation information, if
needed, must be stored separately from study responses. Public artifacts may
contain only de-identified task-level data, broad role categories, and coarsened
experience bands. Free-text responses must be reviewed for identifying content
before release.

The study collects role, programming experience, Python experience, AI-tool
usage, review frequency, task decisions, submitted code, interaction timing,
confidence, workload, and usefulness ratings. Data are used only for the stated
research purpose and retained according to the approved institutional plan.

AI-agent sessions are not human participants. Their outputs are labeled and
stored separately.
