# Participant Information and Consent

## Study

You are invited to participate in a study of software developers reviewing
AI-generated repository code. The session takes approximately 75--90 minutes.
You will inspect twelve Python functions, optionally edit them, and answer short
questions about confidence and workload.

## Risks and Benefits

The study presents no risks beyond ordinary computer use and programming work.
You may experience fatigue or frustration. You may pause or withdraw at any
time without penalty. There is no guaranteed personal benefit.

## Data

We record a pseudonymous participant code, broad professional background,
programming experience, submitted code, task decisions, timing, and ratings.
Do not enter private code, employer information, credentials, or personal data.
De-identified records may be shared for research reproducibility as described
in the approved data-management plan.

## Consent Items

Participants must affirm all items:

- I am at least 18 years old.
- I have read the study information and had an opportunity to ask questions.
- I understand participation is voluntary and I may withdraw.
- I understand what data are collected and how de-identified data may be used.
- I consent to participate.

Study contact, ethics identifier, compensation, and withdrawal procedure must
be inserted after institutional review and before recruitment.
