# Human Study Artifact Status

## Documented Scope

- Preregistered target: 40 completed sessions.
- Documented cohort: 24 uncompensated technical volunteers.
- Workload: 12 scored tasks per participant, or 288 task episodes.
- Conditions: generic review, uncertainty display, and targeted guidance.

## Public Release

- Protocol, consent, questionnaires, schemas, and collection software: present.
- Participant-level responses and identifiers: not released.
- Private stimuli, invitation codes, and private evaluator material: not released.
- Institutional ethics determination: not included.
- Human-condition effect estimates: not reported.

The human component therefore supports protocol transparency, not a public
reanalysis of participant outcomes. Automated-reviewer results are separate and
must not be described as human evidence.
